Finding Your Path in 2035

January 7, 2026 Pravesh Jain Future & Philosophy

You step out of your house. Your phone is your only interface for the system. It is a simple device. You talk to an AI, and it calls a cab. The cab is driverless; the AI simply asks where you want to go. You don’t have to pay. You just go to the station.

In an ideal world, there is a high-speed train waiting. You sit down, travel 2,000 km in five hours, and walk outside. Your luggage is automated because you already told your phone where to send it. Since you were in the cab, the system knows your final destination, and another car is waiting at the exit. It bears your name. (In a truly ideal world, you might take a bus, confident that last-mile connectivity is provided by a cab stationed there). You arrive directly.

"You might have noticed that I didn’t say anything about money. Money is not required to build this; only resources are required."

We trade resources with money, so now we are stuck with it. But the friction it creates is a design flaw, not a necessity.

The Question of Deserving

Do my parents deserve this? Does everyone deserve this?

Yes, everyone does. Not because they did something good in their life, but simply because we can do this. It seems impossible based on the current state, but the ideal state exists. It is just waiting for the architecture to catch up to the technology.
